# 📧 Google Account Email Address Update Procedure

## 📖 Overview

For years, Google did not allow users to change their Gmail address. Once a Gmail address was created, it remained permanently associated with the account.

This created challenges for many users, such as:

* 👤 Creating an email address as a teenager.
* 📝 Using an outdated nickname.
* 💼 Having an email address that no longer appears professional.

Google is now rolling out a new feature that may allow eligible users to change their Gmail address.

This guide explains:

* ✅ How the feature works.
* ✅ What happens to your old Gmail address.
* ✅ How to change your Gmail address (if the feature is available).

---

# ℹ️ What Happens to Your Old Gmail Address?

Many users use their Gmail address for much more than email. It is commonly linked to:

* 🛒 Online shopping websites
* 📱 Social media accounts
* 🎬 Streaming services
* 🏦 Banking applications
* 🔐 Other online services

Because of this, many people are concerned about what happens to their old Gmail address after changing it.

## ✅ Good News

Google does **not** delete your old Gmail address.

Instead:

* Your old Gmail address remains connected to your Google account.
* It becomes an **alternative email address**.
* You can continue receiving emails sent to your old address.
* Your new Gmail address becomes your **primary email address** for signing in and sending/receiving emails.

> **💡 Note:** This feature may not yet be available for every Google account, as it is being rolled out gradually.

---

# 🔄 How to Change Your Gmail Address

Follow these steps to change your Gmail address.

## Step 1 – Open Gmail

1. Open **Gmail**.
2. Sign in to your Google account.

---

## Step 2 – Open Google Account Settings

1. Click your **profile picture** in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Manage your Google Account**.

---

## Step 3 – Open Personal Information

1. From the left navigation menu, click **Personal info**.

---

## Step 4 – Open Email Settings

1. Locate the **Email** section.
2. Click it.

---

## Step 5 – Open Google Account Email

1. Find the option labeled **Google Account Email**.
2. If this feature is available for your account, you will see an arrow next to this option.
3. Click the arrow.

---

## Step 6 – Change Your Email Address

1. Click **Change Google Account Email**.
2. Enter your preferred new Gmail address.

### ⚠️ Requirements

Before proceeding, keep the following in mind:

* The new email address must be available.
* The address must end with **@gmail.com**.
* Carefully read any instructions displayed on the screen.

---

## Step 7 – Confirm the Change

1. If everything looks correct, click **Change Email**.

---

# ✅ Result

Your new Gmail address is now active.

You can now use it to:

* Sign in to your Google account.
* Send and receive emails.
* Continue receiving emails sent to your previous Gmail address, which remains connected as an alternative email address.
